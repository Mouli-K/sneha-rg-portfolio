## Brand & Style

This design system embodies museum-grade minimalism engineered for an executive-tier Product Designer and Creative Technologist. It merges the exacting rigor of Swiss international typography with the warm, tactile restraint of a fine-art monograph.

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Architectural & Deliberate:** Uncluttered, intentional layouts where white space acts as a structural element rather than negative space.
- **Luminous & Calibrated:** Avoiding harsh, cold digital whites (#FFFFFF) in favor of warm stone, chalk, and alabaster tones that soften visual fatigue while preserving clarity.
- **Executive Stature:** Quiet confidence. The design relies on razor-sharp typography scale, exacting alignments, hairline dividers, and nuanced micro-interactions rather than heavy color fills or ornamental graphics.
- **Creative Technologist Duality:** Bridges classical editorial aesthetics with technical fluency through the disciplined integration of monospaced data tags and precise layout grids.

### Visual Language
The system adopts an editorial-minimalist framework accented with frosted crystal translucent tiers. Elements sit on warm layered planes defined by hairline rules (0.5px to 1px) and diffuse ambient lighting rather than heavy, cast drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from archival printing papers, slate inks, and museum exhibition walls. It avoids hyper-saturated primaries and cold sterile grays in favor of warm, mineral-derived values.

### Surface System
- **Base Canvas (`#FBFBFA`):** Warm alabaster, setting a luminous, anti-glare foundation.
- **Subtle Surface (`#F5F5F3`):** Neutral chalk tone for soft container backgrounds, table rows, and secondary regions.
- **Recessed / Structural Surface (`#ECECE8`):** Deep stone for active states, nested pill badges, and elevated rule anchors.
- **Translucent Plane (`rgba(251, 251, 250, 0.82)`): Backdropped with a 16px blur for sticky headers and elevated drawers.

### Ink & Contrast System
- **Primary Ink (`#18181B`):** Deepest charcoal carbon for display headlines, key values, and high-emphasis labels.
- **Secondary Ink (`#27272A`):** Rich slate-zinc for body prose, long-form narratives, and active navigation items.
- **Muted Ink (`#52525B`):** Mid-tone graphite for supporting context, project excerpts, and metadata.
- **Hairline / Subtle Ink (`#71717A`):** Low-emphasis carbon for timestamps, captions, and mono technical specs.

### Accents & Framing
- **Primary Slate Indigo (`#312E81`):** A muted, deep ink-indigo utilized sparingly for interactive focal points, selected states, and hyper-refined text links.
- **Secondary Bronze Taupe (`#8B7355`):** Warm champagne-bronze for featured markers, award indicators, and subtle tag borders.
- **Hairline Border (`#E2E2DF`):** Precise stone rule for 1px spatial boundaries, cards, and editorial separators.

## Typography

Typography establishes the architectural skeleton of the portfolio. The system deploys three distinct typefaces, each fulfilling an explicit editorial role:

1. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Display & Headlines):** Modern, geometric sans-serif with subtle calligraphic roots. Set with tight tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) to convey executive authority, presence, and editorial balance.
2. **Inter (Narrative & Body):** High x-height, neutral, exceptionally legible. Tuned with generous line-heights (1.6 to 1.7) to ensure fatigue-free reading across extensive case study narratives and leadership philosophies.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Metadata, Metrics & Specs):** Monospaced type for system parameters, project dates, role classifications, git hashes, technology stacks, and performance telemetry.

### Typographic Rules
- Always render labels and tags in uppercase when using `label-sm` with wider tracking (`0.06em`).
- Case study headers must pair a `label-md` category indicator above a `headline-lg` title with deliberate vertical rhythm.
- Avoid pure black text on white. The typography relies exclusively on the `#18181B` and `#27272A` values for soft, organic clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column editorial fluid grid with a maximum content container of 1360px. Spacing follows an 8pt structural rhythm, using deliberate whitespace expansions to communicate hierarchy and narrative flow.

### Grid & Breakpoint Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column grid, 64px (`margin`) outer safe margins, 32px (`gutter`) internal gutters. Case studies favor asymmetrical distributions (e.g., 4 columns for sticky contextual metadata, 8 columns for narrative documentation and imagery).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, 32px outer margins, 24px gutters. Secondary sidebars collapse into sequential horizontal blocks.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column grid, 24px (`margin-mobile`) outer margins, 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters. Stacked single-column layouts prioritize linear narrative cadence.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Section Transitions:** Employ large gaps (`space-xl` scaled to 6rem or 8rem in responsive layouts) to punctuate major career epochs and project breakdowns.
- **Micro-Proximity:** Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tag groups, mono captions, and button interior footprints.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy skeuomorphism and muddy dark drop shadows. Depth is achieved via two primary mechanisms: **Tonal Tiering** and **Luminous Glassmorphism**.

### Ambient Shadows
- **Base Level (Resting elements, cards):** No shadow. Elements are defined solely by a 1px border (`#E2E2DF`) and tone shifts from `#FBFBFA` to `#F5F5F3`.
- **Subtle Float (Card hover, floating controls):**
  `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.02)`
  Provides an imperceptible, featherweight lift without introducing visual noise.
- **High Focus (Modal sheets, preview dialogs):**
  `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.03)`

### Glassmorphism & Translucency
Sticky navigation headers, floating audio/prototype control bars, and filtering pills utilize:
- **Surface:** `rgba(251, 251, 250, 0.82)`
- **Backdrop Filter:** `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`
- **Border:** `1px solid rgba(226, 226, 223, 0.7)`
This produces a frosted, archival glass effect where background elements softly blur without color distortion.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** geometry profile to maintain an architectural, tailored persona. Curvature is restrained, resisting overly playful or bubbly radii in favor of precision corners.

### Radius Scale
- **Small Components (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Checkboxes, inline code badges, status indicators, and technical indicators.
- **Interactive Triggers & Inputs (`0.375rem` - `0.5rem` / 6px - 8px):** Primary buttons, text fields, menu containers, and dropdown lists.
- **Containers & Project Cards (`0.5rem` - `0.75rem` / 8px - 12px):** Case study imagery cards, hero frames, and timeline milestones.
- **Pill Exception (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators (e.g., "Available for Advisory Q3"), interactive tag filters, and compact mono chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#18181B` fill with `#FBFBFA` text. 40px height, 6px border-radius, `Inter` 14px weight 500. Padding: 0 18px. Hover state translates Y by -1px with a muted transition to `#27272A`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a 1px border (`#E2E2DF`). Ink text (`#18181B`). Hover transitions background to `#F5F5F3` with zero layout shift.
- **Ghost / Editorial Link:** No border or background. Understated underline offset by 4px with a 1px stroke of `#8B7355` (Champagne Bronze).

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Built using `JetBrains Mono` at `label-sm` (11px).
- Upper-case text, 4px vertical padding, 10px horizontal padding.
- Border: 1px hairline (`#E2E2DF`) on a `#F5F5F3` surface.
- Dedicated technology tags use slate indigo tints (`rgba(49, 46, 129, 0.06)`) with `#312E81` text.

### Portfolio & Case Study Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp 1px `#E2E2DF` outer frame, `#F5F5F3` interior image canvas with an aspect ratio of 16:10.
- **Hover State:** Border transitions to `#71717A` over 300ms cubic-bezier (0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). Images employ an internal scale of 1.02.
- **Metadata Placement:** Mono timestamp and category sit at top right; title and excerpt sit directly below the frame on the `#FBFBFA` surface.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FBFBFA` with a 1px outline in `#E2E2DF`.
- Height: 42px. Typography: `Inter` 14px. Placeholder text in `#71717A`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#312E81` with a soft outer glow of `0 0 0 2px rgba(49, 46, 129, 0.08)`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- 16px square/circle with a 1px `#E2E2DF` border.
- Checked state: `#18181B` fill with a crisp white hairline checkmark or concentric dot.

### Specialized Components
- **Executive Metric Block:** A high-impact numeric container (`headline-xl` in `#18181B`) grounded by a `label-sm` description and a 1px `#E2E2DF` top rule.
- **Code & Spec Snippet:** Inline terminal-style block on `#F5F5F3` bordered in `#E2E2DF`, utilizing `JetBrains Mono` with subtle syntax highlighting in indigo, slate, and bronze.
